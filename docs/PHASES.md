# HLS Aurora — living phases

The ship is not a museum. A watch is a dramatic unit. Time passing must be felt.

Ship clock starts at 06:00:00 and runs faster than wall time. Facility clock stays 49 minutes ahead. Do not reconcile them.

| Phase | Ship time from 06:00 | Crew name | Observer name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wake | 0–12 min | Wake | Open-state |
| walkdown | 12–32 min | Walkdown | Floor |
| midwatch | 32–64 min | Midwatch | Mid-session |
| quiet | 64+ min | Quiet hours | Hold |

What must change with phase

- Clock faces read live time.
- Light. Wake cooler. Midwatch held. Quiet dims.
- People. Midwatch they walk more. Quiet they pause longer. Observer still freezes the floor.
- Radio. One line at each phase boundary, dual-skinned. Never spam.
- Residue. Pull still writes on the crew world. Phases do not wipe it.

Nearby speech is one line per person per phase. Observer lines are shorter and colder. Unknown ids stay quiet.

Do not

- Freeze the ship clock as the only time the player sees.
- Advance story by loading a new map. Same tiles, later watch.
- Combat, jump-scares, or a timer that fails the player. The clock is weather, not a score.
